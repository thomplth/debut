Command hint directions

Twenty treatments of the same overlay and the same concise, action-first language. Each direction unifies the classic command hints with the top desktop indicator. Click any screenshot to inspect the full-resolution capture.

Captured in the headless Tart VM 2880 × 1800 each 3 stages · 9 windows Unified top indicator + hints 20 directions
01

Bare Legend

Text floats beside the plates with almost no chrome, making the overlay itself the dominant object.

Tradeoff: quietest option, but more dependent on text shadow and the desktop behind it.

02

Soft Glass

Subtle translucent capsules echo the plate material without competing with window content.

Tradeoff: feels native and cohesive, though the smallest labels become restrained.

03

Nested Keycaps

Plain-language actions sit beside compact shortcut keycaps, separating meaning from input at a glance.

Tradeoff: clearest command grammar, with moderately more visual weight.

04

Unified Dock

All bottom actions share one continuous control strip while stage and window hints remain anchored locally.

Tradeoff: exceptionally orderly, but reads more like a toolbar than annotation.

05

Accent Categories

Color assigns quick identities to navigation, movement, quitting, and closing.

Tradeoff: fastest to scan and most expressive, but intentionally less neutral.

06

Two-Line Tiles

A micro-label names the action above a larger shortcut, giving both pieces equal structure.

Tradeoff: highly legible hierarchy, at the cost of a taller footprint.

07

Outline Brackets

Open corner brackets frame each hint without introducing another filled surface.

Tradeoff: distinctive and airy, but the most stylized direction in the set.

08

Editorial

Warm parchment tones and rounded typography make the guidance feel like marginal notes.

Tradeoff: approachable and memorable, while deliberately departing from the cool overlay palette.

09

Status Dots

Small semantic dots add scanning landmarks to otherwise restrained dark pills.

Tradeoff: compact with a touch of character, though dot meaning is learned rather than literal.

10

High Contrast

Bright labels with inset black keys maximize separation from both plates and desktop content.

Tradeoff: strongest readability, and also the loudest visual presence.

11

Command Palette

Dark action rows use a colored prompt and right-aligned shortcut, borrowing the efficiency of searchable command menus.

Tradeoff: excellent information structure, but more rectangular and tool-like.

12

Blueprint

Deep technical blue, cyan outlines, and monospaced type frame the controls as a precise operating diagram.

Tradeoff: crisp and systematic, with a deliberately specialized character.

13

Neon Circuit

Colored luminous outlines make each control category pop against the desktop and translucent plates.

Tradeoff: instantly discoverable and energetic, though intentionally game-like.

14

Paper Tags

Lightweight white labels, serif text, and a slight physical tilt read like annotations pinned to the interface.

Tradeoff: human and quiet, but less native to the surrounding glass language.

15

Signal Lights

Compact lettered color signals identify stage, window, action, and display controls within neutral capsules.

Tradeoff: adds reliable scan landmarks, with a small visual code to learn.

16

Minimal Rule

Text remains almost entirely unboxed while a thin semantic underline supplies just enough anchoring.

Tradeoff: the lightest structured treatment, but sensitive to busy backgrounds.

17

Frosted Labels

Two-level typography inside generous frosted capsules gives action names and shortcuts a calm hierarchy.

Tradeoff: polished and readable, at the cost of a slightly larger footprint.

18

Terminal

Green monospaced commands, prompt marks, and bracketed inputs turn the overlay into a tiny command-line legend.

Tradeoff: highly compact and unmistakable, but aimed at a technical aesthetic.

19

Warm Gradient

Coral-to-magenta capsules create one bold visual layer shared by every command and the top indicator.

Tradeoff: friendly and exceptionally visible, while carrying the strongest brand color.

20

Segmented Light

A fine divider separates each plain-language action from its shortcut inside a crisp light control.

Tradeoff: explicit and accessible, though visually firmer than translucent options.

These are exploration captures, not production selections. The shipped command hints remain unchanged until a direction is chosen.
