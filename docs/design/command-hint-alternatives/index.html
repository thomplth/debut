Command hint directions

Ten treatments of the same overlay and the same concise, action-first language. Click any screenshot to inspect the full-resolution capture.

Captured in the headless Tart VM 2880 × 1800 each 3 stages · 9 windows Production UI fixture
01

Bare Legend

Text floats beside the plates with almost no chrome, making the overlay itself the dominant object.

Tradeoff: quietest option, but more dependent on text shadow and the desktop behind it.

02

Soft Glass

Subtle translucent capsules echo the plate material without competing with window content.

Tradeoff: feels native and cohesive, though the smallest labels become restrained.

03

Nested Keycaps

Plain-language actions sit beside compact shortcut keycaps, separating meaning from input at a glance.

Tradeoff: clearest command grammar, with moderately more visual weight.

04

Unified Dock

All bottom actions share one continuous control strip while stage and window hints remain anchored locally.

Tradeoff: exceptionally orderly, but reads more like a toolbar than annotation.

05

Accent Categories

Color assigns quick identities to navigation, movement, quitting, and closing.

Tradeoff: fastest to scan and most expressive, but intentionally less neutral.

06

Two-Line Tiles

A micro-label names the action above a larger shortcut, giving both pieces equal structure.

Tradeoff: highly legible hierarchy, at the cost of a taller footprint.

07

Outline Brackets

Open corner brackets frame each hint without introducing another filled surface.

Tradeoff: distinctive and airy, but the most stylized direction in the set.

08

Editorial

Warm parchment tones and rounded typography make the guidance feel like marginal notes.

Tradeoff: approachable and memorable, while deliberately departing from the cool overlay palette.

09

Status Dots

Small semantic dots add scanning landmarks to otherwise restrained dark pills.

Tradeoff: compact with a touch of character, though dot meaning is learned rather than literal.

10

High Contrast

Bright labels with inset black keys maximize separation from both plates and desktop content.

Tradeoff: strongest readability, and also the loudest visual presence.

These are exploration captures, not production selections. The shipped command hints remain unchanged until a direction is chosen.
